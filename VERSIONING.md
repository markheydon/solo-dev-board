## Overview

[SoloDevBoard](README.md) follows [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/) for public releases. Build-time versions are calculated by [MinVer](https://github.com/adamralph/minver) on `SoloDevBoard.App`.

Release process, staging versus production tiers, and roadmap milestones are documented in [`plan/RELEASE_PLAN.md`](plan/RELEASE_PLAN.md) and [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
